## Cross-platform posting across four separate accounts on one Mac, from one app

If you use Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, and Mastodon, posting the same update separately means switching between accounts and posting your draft four times. Mima's cross-platform posting lets you write an update once and share it across those networks.

![The Mima composer with a short message typed in and the Bluesky, X, Mastodon and LinkedIn toggles all switched on](https://mima.social/site-assets/app/compose-typed-desk.webp "Write once, with all four networks switched on. People and posts in these pictures are made up.")

Mima also lets you reply to your own cross-posts from the same composer, making threads and "link in reply" type posting across four networks a breeze. Additionally, as people reply to your posts on different networks, you can reply to them from one place, without leaving Mima.

![The composer replying to your own cross-post: a box shows “Replying to” with the original post, a link is typed in, and all four networks are selected](https://mima.social/site-assets/app/omni-reply.webp "Replying to your own cross-post adds the reply to every copy you choose: handy for a link in reply.")

On an Apple Silicon Mac, [Mima](https://mima.social) supports all four networks. You connect your existing accounts using each network’s own login, then compose once to share across them. Mima also brings your chronological feeds and inbox into its desktop app, so you can read posts and handle replies without returning to four separate apps.

## How Mima's compose-once workflow actually works

To cross-post to Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, and Mastodon, first connect each account in [Mima](https://mima.social).

- **You do not need a Mima account.** Mima is software that runs on your machine, so it doesn't require any additional accounts to use. (Once upon a time, software did not require accounts to run locally!)
- **Mima does not store your passwords** and keeps you signed in on your computer.
- **You sign in with each network's own login page.** For Bluesky and Mastodon, Mima opens the sign-in page in your usual web browser. For X and LinkedIn, the network's own login page opens in a Mima window.

![Mima Settings, Accounts tab, listing connected Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Mastodon accounts, each with a Disconnect button](https://mima.social/site-assets/app/accounts-card.webp "Settings, then Accounts: one account per network, all four connected.")

After connecting the accounts, you can begin to compose messages once and share them across any of your networks. Data travels directly between your social accounts and the app, without routing through any Mima servers.

## Checking your posts after publishing: feed and inbox

Your new post shows up in Mima's News Wall as one card, with a badge for each network it went to. While it is still sending, the card is greyed out and marked "Posting…". If a network refuses the post, the card is marked "Not sent" with the reason, and you can retry it, edit it in the composer, or discard it.

![The News Wall showing three of your posts: one greyed out and marked Posting…, one marked Not sent with Retry, Edit and Discard buttons, and one sent to all four networks with replies and likes](https://mima.social/site-assets/app/post-status.webp "Sending, not sent, and sent to all four networks.")

The News Wall shows the people you follow in time order, and you can sort it by newest, most active or least active. As replies come in, the unified inbox brings mentions and direct messages from your connected networks into one place, with pins and reminders for follow-up. To answer replies to a cross-post, reply once and choose which networks' copies to reply to.

![The News Wall sort menu open, with Newest, Most active and Least active](https://mima.social/site-assets/app/sort-menu.webp "The sort menu on the News Wall.")

## What Mima does not do: scheduling, analytics, and unsupported networks

Mima supports cross-posting to [Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, and Mastodon](https://mima.social). It does not support Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok. Tell us which networks you'd like to see next!

### When a scheduler or agency tool fits better than compose-and-publish

Mima’s [compose-and-publish workflow](https://mima.social) fits when you want to write a post on your Mac and publish it yourself. If posts must go live while you are away, or clients must approve drafts before publication, look for a scheduler or agency tool built around those steps.

Approval needs depend on who can publish. [BskyGrowth’s small-team guidance](https://blog.bskygrowth.com/social-media-approval-workflow-small-team/) says a solo operator posting under their own name may not need formal review. It suggests that two or three people can use a shared checklist, while a third regular contributor who needs a separate reviewer role may justify a paid approval tool.

Multi-client work adds another coordination problem. [Schedulala describes](https://schedulala.com/blog/social-media-workflow-agency-best-practices) agencies losing approvals in message threads and risking posts to the wrong account as client counts grow. Both sources offer general workflow advice, not evidence of a specific Mima limit.

## FAQ

**Which Mac hardware do I need?** An Apple Silicon Mac.

**Do I need a new Mima account?** No. You only need logins for the networks you want to connect (X, Bluesky, Mastodon, LinkedIn).

**Does Mima store my passwords?** No. When you sign into your account, Mima preserves the connection's sessions, not your password.

**Can I schedule posts?** Not at this time.
