# AI agents

> Connect Claude, Codex and other AI agents to your posts and conversations through Mima's local MCP server.

Source: https://www.mima.social/learn/topics/ai-agents/ · Part of https://www.mima.social/learn/index.md

- [What's an MCP Server for Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Mastodon?](https://www.mima.social/learn/mcp-server-bluesky-x-linkedin-mastodon.md): Mima includes a local, read-only MCP server, so Claude Desktop, Codex, Goose and other AI agents on your Mac can search and summarize your posts, mentions and DMs from Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Mastodon. (Updated 2026-11-23.)

---

About Mima: Mima is a free desktop app (beta, Apple Silicon Macs) with one feed of the people you follow on Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Mastodon, one composer for posting to several networks, and one inbox for conversations.
