# Comparisons

> How Mima compares with other ways to manage several social accounts, and when another tool fits better.

Source: https://mima.social/learn/topics/comparisons/ · Part of https://mima.social/learn/index.md

- [Private Social Account Managers: Local Desktop vs Self-Hosted Tools](https://mima.social/learn/private-social-account-managers.md): Mima is a local Mac app for following people and replying across Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, and Mastodon; Mixpost is a self-hosted or cloud scheduler; Flare is an open-source client with more networks. (Updated 2026-11-10.)

About Mima: Mima is a free desktop app (beta, Apple Silicon Macs) with one feed of the people you follow on Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Mastodon, one composer for posting to several networks, and one inbox for conversations.
