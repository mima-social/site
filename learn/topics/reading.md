# Reading

> Read everyone you follow in one calm feed, and narrow it to the lists you already keep on Bluesky and Mastodon.

Source: https://www.mima.social/learn/topics/reading/ · Part of https://www.mima.social/learn/index.md

- [Can I Read My Bluesky and Mastodon Lists in One App?](https://www.mima.social/learn/bluesky-mastodon-lists-one-app.md): Yes. Mima imports the lists you made on Bluesky and Mastodon and shows them beside its Timeline on your Mac, so one click narrows your feed to the people on a list. (Updated 2026-11-23.)

---

About Mima: Mima is a free desktop app (beta, Apple Silicon Macs) with one feed of the people you follow on Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Mastodon, one composer for posting to several networks, and one inbox for conversations.
