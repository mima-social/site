# Learn: keep up with your people, not the algorithms

> Plain guides from the makers of Mima to following friends, posting and keeping conversations going across Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Mastodon. Each guide links to its Markdown version.

Source: https://www.mima.social/learn/

## Latest guides

- [What's an MCP Server for Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Mastodon?](https://www.mima.social/learn/mcp-server-bluesky-x-linkedin-mastodon.md): Mima includes a local, read-only MCP server, so Claude Desktop, Codex, Goose and other AI agents on your Mac can search and summarize your posts, mentions and DMs from Bluesky, X, LinkedIn and Mastodon. (Updated 2026-11-23.)
- [Can I Read My Bluesky and Mastodon Lists in One App?](https://www.mima.social/learn/bluesky-mastodon-lists-one-app.md): Yes. Mima imports the lists you made on Bluesky and Mastodon and shows them beside its Timeline on your Mac, so one click narrows your feed to the people on a list. (Updated 2026-11-23.)
- [Private Unified Social Inbox for Mac: Manage Messages and Mentions with Mima](https://www.mima.social/learn/private-unified-social-inbox-mac.md): Mima brings mentions and DMs from Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, and Mastodon into one private inbox on your Mac, where you can filter, reply, pin conversations, set reminders, and catch up with optional Thread Summaries. (Updated 2026-11-23.)
- [Private Social Account Managers: Local Desktop vs Self-Hosted Tools](https://www.mima.social/learn/private-social-account-managers.md): Mima is a local Mac app for following people and replying across Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, and Mastodon; Mixpost is a self-hosted or cloud scheduler; Flare is an open-source client with more networks. (Updated 2026-11-10.)
- [How to Cross-Post to Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, and Mastodon from a Mac](https://www.mima.social/learn/cross-post-bluesky-x-linkedin-mastodon-mac.md): On an Apple Silicon Mac, Mima lets you sign in to Bluesky, X, LinkedIn, and Mastodon with each network’s own login, then compose once and publish across all four accounts. (Updated 2026-11-23.)
