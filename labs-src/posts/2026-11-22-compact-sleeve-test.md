This is a test post. It checks the compact sleeve, which a post asks for with `compact: true` in its front matter.

A show-and-tell post is mostly its picture or recording, with a few sentences around it. The compact sleeve gives it a smaller title and puts the picture at full width, so a short post does not look over-packaged. The credits band is the same as on a long post.
